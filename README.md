# RealAItor

> *"Claude, list my house for sale. Get me the most money possible. Make no mistakes."*
>
> That's what I typed. And then I hit enter.

---

## What Is This?

RealAItor is the public documentation of a real-world experiment: **using AI to support as much of a home sale as possible while keeping human judgment in the loop.**

---

## The Process

Selling a house touches: market research, legal compliance, pricing strategy, negotiation, marketing, and closing. It's a perfect test bed — high stakes, multiple skill domains, involves licensed professionals, and has a clear outcome (did it sell, and for how much?).

I'm using AI tools, including [Claude](https://claude.ai), to assist across every phase. I'll document:

- ✅ What AI did well
- ❌ Where it fell short
- 🤝 Where I still needed a human
- 📊 The final scorecard vs. AI predictions

---

## What's In This Repo

```
/skills          ← The actual Claude skills powering this experiment
/social          ← Published posts and content drafts
/research        ← Sanitized market research and methodology
```

### Skills

The `skills/` directory contains the Claude skill files (SKILL.md) used throughout this process. These are reusable — if you're doing something similar, fork them.

- **`selling-house-marketing-content/`** — Listing copy, social content, brand voice
- **`researching-philadelphia-real-estate/`** — Comps analysis, market research workflow

> ⚠️ Note: Skills contain project context (neighborhood, property type, timeline). Adapt them to your own situation.

### What's NOT in this repo

Sensitive financial details, exact offer amounts, and closing documents stay private. The methodology and skills are public. The bank account isn't.
